Industries Served.

Cloud Focal provides specialized technology consulting, custom systems integration, and technical talent acquisition tailored to the operational demands and regulatory environments of both Public Sector agencies and Private Enterprises.

Public Sector

Cloud Focal partners with Australian Federal and State Government departments, local councils, and international public sector entities to deliver resilient digital public services, sovereign cloud migrations, and hardened cybersecurity architectures.

We navigate complex compliance landscapes and procurement frameworks, ensuring taxpayer funds deliver maximum long-term efficiency and citizen impact:

  • Australian Frameworks: Alignment with ACSC Essential Eight, PSPF, and IRAP certification readiness
  • Sovereign Cloud & GOTS: Deployment of Government Off-The-Shelf systems on protected cloud enclaves
  • Citizen Service Modernization: Accessible, multi-channel citizen engagement portals compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA
  • Vetted Clearances: Technical staffing and engineering squads holding necessary national security clearances

Private Enterprise

From ASX-listed corporations and multinational enterprises to high-growth venture-backed companies, we provide the technical leverage necessary to outpace market competitors, streamline operational workflows, and reduce infrastructure spend.

Our cross-functional teams bring deep domain expertise across financial services, healthcare, logistics, telecommunications, and retail:

  • Financial Services & FinTech: Low-latency data pipelines, APRA CPS 234 compliance, and PCI-DSS architecture
  • Healthcare & MedTech: Secure patient data platforms compliant with Australian Health Records & HIPAA standards
  • Retail & eCommerce: High-throughput omnichannel architectures capable of handling peak festive volumes
  • Logistics & Supply Chain: Real-time IoT telematics, predictive analytics, and automated tracking systems

Bridging the gap between strategy and execution. Melbourne-headquartered technical staffing and digital consulting partner serving Australian and international enterprise clients.
