Cookie Policy

Learn how Cloud Focal utilizes cookies and local storage tokens. View our cookie inventory matrix and manage your preferences in compliance with APPs, GDPR, and CCPA.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to maintain session state, secure forms, store user preferences, and collect anonymous telemetry on website performance.

2. Cookie Categories

Strictly Necessary (Always Active)

Enables core security, CSRF protection, session continuity, and consent recording. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics & Performance

Measures visitor engagement and page speeds via Google Analytics with IP anonymization.

Functional & Preferences

Preserves UI configurations such as theme toggle settings and saved job search filters.

Marketing & Targeting

Attributes technical talent recruitment campaigns across platforms such as LinkedIn.

4. Browser-Level Controls & GPC Signals

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Cloud Focal actively recognizes and honors Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.
